## Design System & Aesthetics
- **Direction:** Architectural Editorial Monograph (warm alabaster paper `#F6F5F0`, crisp white elevated cards `#FFFFFF`, carbon ink `#141517`, oxidized terracotta accent `#B84A27`, hairline borders `rgba(20, 21, 23, 0.10)`).
- **Layout:** Clean `max-w-[1200px] mx-auto px-6 md:px-10` container alignment across header, footer, and all public pages (`/`, `/work`, `/work/[slug]`, `/about`, `/skills`, `/contact`).
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk (`--font-display`), Inter (`--font-sans`), JetBrains Mono (`--font-mono`), and Playfair Display (`--font-serif` for restrained italic header accents).
- **Photography & Screenshots:** Full-color, zero grayscale/shader filters. Studio portrait `/viswa.jpg` sits inside a cool-stone mat (`--bg-portrait-mat: #E4E6E9`).
- **No Gimmicks:** All WebGL/Three.js shaders (`DitherBackground`, `DistortionImage`, `ParticleField`), GSAP text splitters/preloaders, custom cursor listeners, and unverified placeholder projects (`PropChain`) have been permanently removed.
